## What they all miss (our additions)
1. **First split: one tap vs every tap**, with the mixing-valve cause sourced to the A. O. Smith manual.
2. **Flowchart SVG + check table by type** (electric, gas standing pilot, gas electronic control, tankless, one tap only).
3. **Recovery times** (gas 30–40 min, electric 60–90 min) and the manual's "wait two hours after lighting" rule.
4. **"Some hot water but not enough" causes** from the manual (mixing valve, undersized, reversed connections or melted dip tube, hot-side plumbing leak, sediment).
5. **"No hot water after a flush"** (element burned out by powering an empty tank).
6. **Embedded repair-or-replace calculator** and links to the size calculators.
7. **No gas or wiring repair steps** beyond what the manufacturer tells owners (CLAUDE.md §8b), unlike Bob Vila's thermocouple how-to.
