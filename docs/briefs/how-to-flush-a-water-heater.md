## What they all miss (our additions)
1. **Manufacturer schedule table** by type and water (first flush at six months, then yearly; twice-yearly service with hard water; softened water and the anode), sourced to the A. O. Smith manual, GE and Rinnai, instead of an unsourced "every six months".
2. **Steps that follow the manufacturer manual exactly**, including the three-minute air purge and "never power or light an empty tank".
3. **Setup diagram (SVG)** of valves, faucet and hose.
4. **Old, never-flushed tank** answered honestly (no maker forbids it; clogged valve → qualified person) with the age calculator embedded.
5. **Tankless descaling** with Rinnai's figures (4 gallons undiluted white vinegar, no chemicals, up to 45 minutes, inlet filter from TB-188).
6. **Common mistakes list** tied to manual warnings.
