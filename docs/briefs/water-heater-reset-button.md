## What they all miss (our additions)
1. **Two manufacturers disagree, shown side by side:** A. O. Smith lets owners press it with the power off; GE says only a qualified technician should reset it. We give both and our reading.
2. **Sourced causes table** from A. O. Smith's electric manual (shorted element, thermostat stuck or not tight, no click = ECO not tripped, relief valve dumping very hot water = shorted element).
3. **Breaker vs reset button comparison table.**
4. **Gas heaters:** the button by the knob is the igniter (A. O. Smith lighting label); FVIR resettable thermal switch must never be disabled.
5. **SVG diagram** of where the ECO sits (upper thermostat only).
6. Honest note: the "power surge" cause found on blogs has no manufacturer source, and the trip temperature is not in current manuals, so we give none.
7. Embedded repair-or-replace calculator.
