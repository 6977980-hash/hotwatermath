## What they all miss (our additions)
1. **A verdict for your number** (below / fair low / fair high / above / far above) for your state, heater and extras.
2. **Line-by-line check** of unit, labor and permit against sourced ranges.
3. **What explains a high quote** (extras ticked) and three worked example quotes.
4. **Consumer rules from state agencies** (Virginia DPOR, New York AG): three written estimates, small deposit, final payment after inspection.
5. **No lead-gen:** we do not pass the quote to anyone.

## How we are different from generic calculator clones
Uses our published, DOE-checked cost model; the one editorial rule (far above = more than 50% over our high) is disclosed on /methodology.
